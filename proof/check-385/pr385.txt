# 385: agent: The listener publishes exactly one discovery record
state: open

Fixes #349.

## What changes

The agent listener publishes exactly one discovery record, the right one. Before, the listener could publish duplicate or stale discovery records, so an external client (the way `bir-mcp` is one) could connect to the wrong record or see more than one. Touches `crates/bir-app-api/src/agent/headless.rs`, `crates/bir-app-api/src/agent/mod.rs` and `nsh/src/agent.rs`, rebased from f7d87513 onto current main.
