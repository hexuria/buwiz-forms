Why your typing disappears
The app writes your digits into a box that does not exist yet, gets told no, and puts the old empty value back.
you type 08→wait 0.3 s→draft has no due_date→box reset to empty

A new 0605 starts with no due date at all, and the writer can only fill a date that already exists. Same thing hits Item 6 ATC, Item 7 Return Period and Item 8 Tax Type. The calendar pick fails the same way on a new return.

Why three boxes share one error
Each refused box adds its own message to the item's one message line, then the form adds "Invalid date entry on item 4."

They are not three checks. It is one line per item, with every refusal glued on with a dot, in reverse order, using internal names like /due_date/year.

Why line of business is asked 4 times
The box, the form's own rule, a profile check, and a card with a second box and Save all point at the same answer.

Typing in the box fills the return but never the profile, and the card stays until you reopen the return. 41 of 43 forms can show such cards.

Why tests stayed green
No test ever typed into a split date or a 0605 field.

The Rust "every date box" test skips split boxes; samples always start with a date filled; 19 of 43 forms are only opened, never typed into; 7 of 8 select tests return early headless.
